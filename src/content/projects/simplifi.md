Prototyped a web application that would save UK farmers on the order of thousands of pounds a year with environmental scheme compliance. Centralised task and field management together with evidence compilation, removing manual efforts to collate and distribute the required information.

Effectively utilised the agile methodology, working closely with farmers nationwide from project definition through to prototype. Validated incremental design choices at each stage, incrementally creating intuitive workflows, leading to high stakeholder satisfaction by the end of the project.

Developed a Next.js web app in TypeScript, utilising the Prisma ORM for database interaction.

Recognised as the 4th best project out of 56 in the year.
