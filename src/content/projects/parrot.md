Developed a scalable pronunciation training app by integrating ensemble ML models (Allosaurus and Whisper) for real-time phoneme inference.

Engineered a Dockerized FastAPI backend with an NGINX load balancer to auto-scale inference microservices across multiple languages.

Optimized model performance by adopting an instance-per-call strategy for Allosaurus, reducing latency and improving throughput.

Leveraged AWS CloudFront CDN to deliver phoneme recordings, cutting global response times by over 67%.

Made extensive use of Scrum ceremonies, with sprint planning and retrospectives proving particularly helpful in refining workflows and ensuring project alignment.

Awarded the David Howarth Group Project Prize for best overall group project
